# AI/ML Interview Scoring Rubric

Each answer is scored **0–5** on four categories (max total **20**).

## Clarifying questions
Did the candidate surface unknowns before solving? Did they ask about the
business goal, the data (volume, labels, quality), constraints (latency, cost,
privacy), and success criteria instead of assuming?

## Problem framing
Did they turn a vague goal into a well-defined ML task — target, inputs, a
simple baseline first, then a sequenced path to something more complex?

## Metrics & evaluation
Did they pick the right metric for the problem (class imbalance, cost of errors,
offline vs online), a sound validation split, and name the main failure modes —
leakage, drift, hallucination — and how they'd monitor for them?

## Communication
Was the answer structured, concise, and pitched at the listener — could a
non-technical stakeholder follow the reasoning and the trade-offs?

---

**Score guide per category:** 0 = absent · 1–2 = weak · 3 = solid · 4 = strong ·
5 = exceptional.
