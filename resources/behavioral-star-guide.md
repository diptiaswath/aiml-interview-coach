# Guide: The Behavioral (STAR+) Round

AI/ML behavioral rounds probe ownership, handling ambiguity, and communicating
technical results to non-technical people.

**STAR+ structure**
- **Situation** — brief context.
- **Task** — what you owned.
- **Action** — what *you* did (not the team).
- **Result** — the measurable outcome.
- **+ Reflection** — what you learned / would do differently.

**Signals they want:** owning a model that underperformed in production; explaining
a result or a limitation credibly to stakeholders; making a call with imperfect
data.
